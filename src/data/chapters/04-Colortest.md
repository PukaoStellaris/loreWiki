> Location: Nowhere in particular — the space between the page and whoever's holding it

The chapter break hits different this time. Nobody in the room moves toward the door. Instead, one by one, they turn, toward you.

:::dialogue
> Sentinel taps the scar at her temple, the old tell, but this time it looks almost like she's deciding whether you're worth the honesty.

Sentinel Phantasma: So you're the one who's been reading over my shoulder this whole time.

Sentinel Phantasma (grinning): Relax. I'm not going to pretend I didn't notice. You've seen me at my worst, the empty stuff, the stuff I don't say out loud to anyone but Astral.

> A beat, quieter.

Sentinel Phantasma: Since you're already this far in: no illusion, no disguise, not even for you. Keep reading. I'd rather you know the real version.

> Astral sets her teacup down and considers you the way she considers everything. Carefully, like you might be worth more thought than you've given yourself credit for.

Astral Anemos: You've been here longer than most of the people in this room, in a way. Watching. Not interfering, which, frankly, shows more discipline than half my officers.

Astral Anemos (real faintly smiles): I won't insult you by pretending this is 'just a story.' It's ours. You're simply the one it was handed to. Read carefully. Some of us are still deciding how it ends.

> Stellium studies you for a long moment, arms crossed, the way she used to study Sentinel before deciding whether she'd earned a hard truth yet.

Stellium Choros: You already know about Durandal. You already know what it cost me. 

Stellium Choros: I don't soften things for the people in this room, and I'm not going to start with you. If you're still here after everything you've read, the losses, all of it, that tells me something about what kind of reader you are. Good. Keep going. It gets harder before it gets better.

Vesta looks almost giddy at the idea of you, hands immediately reaching for a notebook that isn't there.

Vesta Aeris: Wait... you've been keeping track of all of this? All of it, from the beginning?

Vesta Aeris (delighted, laughs): That's... that's a documentation project bigger than mine, honestly. Do you know how many casualty reports I've filed since page one? Never mind, don't answer that, I don't think either of us wants the real number.

Vesta Aeris (softer): Thank you for staying. It means someone besides me was counting.

> Zane flips his coin, watching you instead of it, and, notably, doesn't stop it spinning. Not yet.

Zane Axios: You're the reader. Huh. Didn't expect an audience for the parts I only ever say out loud to myself.

> The coin slows in his palm. 
Zane Axios: Partner's Log: reader is real, apparently, and has been here the whole time. 

Zane Axios (smirks): Don't tell Sentinel I said this part was fine by me.

> Sullivan regards you the way he regards a negotiation, assessing exactly what you already know, and what that's worth.

Sullivan Domineer: You have seen both versions of me. The one who dealt in leverage and disappeared before the cost came due, and the one standing here now. 
Sullivan Domineer: I will not ask what you thought of the first one. I already know. What I will say is this: you kept reading past it. That is not nothing. Most people close the book on someone like who I was.

> Mirai leans against the doorframe, looking at you with the same quiet wonder she once reserved for her own unexpected competence.

Lyra Mirai: You've been with me since I was terrified and yelling insults at things twice my size. 
Lyra Mirai (laughing): I still don't totally believe I made it this far... and somehow you believed it before I did, just by staying to find out. 
> She tilts her head, warm.
Lyra Mirai: So... thank you. Really. Not many people stick around for someone else's whole becoming.

> A pause settles over the room. Then Sentinel looks back at you one last time, the grin returning in full.

Sentinel Phantasma: Well? Go on, then. 
> She nods toward the next page, wherever it is for you. 
Sentinel Phantasma: We're not finished yet.
:::
