Every mage in the Kingdom knew the number: thirty-three seconds. The exact duration Sentinel Phantasma could hold the {void}Void{/} before it began holding *her*. The scroll that gave her Nihil had never promised mercy, and Oreia — the goddess sleeping inside her mana — collected interest.

Luna changed the arithmetic.

## The Experiment

It began as an accident in the training yard: Luna's {luna}Aethereal light{/} brushing against her sister's {sentinel}violet corruption{/} — and instead of recoiling, the two forces *braided*.

:::dialogue
Sentinel: Again.
Luna: You've been holding it for four minutes, Sen.
Sentinel: I know. **Again.**
Luna (laughing): You could at least pretend to be tired!
Sentinel: I have spent six years pretending the ground under me was about to open. Let me have this.
> The violet in her eyes did not flicker. It burned steady, like a lamp finally trimmed.
Luna: ...as long as I'm beside you, it doesn't end at thirty-three. It doesn't end at all.
:::

The war room named it the *Dual Star system* — the official notation was "S\{L\}" in Xi's ledgers, which everyone agreed looked more like a cat than a formula.

---

When the Monarch of the Dark Continent finally moved against the Spire, it met a Sentinel with no timer over her head. What happened next did not leave ruins to study. The Monarch was not defeated, not sealed, not banished.

It was {crimson}deleted{/} — removed from existence entirely, and the Dark Continent woke the next morning with no ruler and no memory of why the sky felt lighter.
