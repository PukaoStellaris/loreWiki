The Citadel bells rang at midnight, and for the first time in three hundred years, they rang for one of their own.

Word traveled through the marble halls faster than any courier: the Archon had ordered the arrest of {sentinel}Sentinel Phantasma{/}, the Kingdom's Ace, the Violet Nihility herself. Her crime was simple. She had moved to protect her younger sister — and the Archon had decided that loyalty to blood outweighed loyalty to the crown.

By dawn, every Cardinal of the Divinity Council had laid their commission on the Archon's desk. Ten resignations, ten empty seats, and one furious old man staring at the ruin of his own decree.

## The Gates

{astral}Astral Anemos{/} was waiting at the western gate when the escort brought her through. He hadn't slept. The wind around him hadn't stopped moving since the bells.

:::dialogue
Sentinel: You shouldn't be here. They'll call this treason.
Astral (grinning): They already did. I have the paperwork somewhere.
> Behind them, torchlight gathered on the inner walls — the garrison, assembling.
Sentinel: Luna. Where is Luna?
Astral: Safe. Xi took her through the aqueducts an hour ago. We're the last ones out.
Sentinel: ...you resigned. All of you. For me.
Astral: For *us*. If you can't keep up with the elements, force them to bow to you — you taught me that. The Archon forgot it.
:::

They went over the wall as the bells changed rhythm — no longer a summons, but an alarm.

---

What left the Citadel that night was not a fugitive and her rescuers. It was the founding of something new: a rogue coalition the broadsheets would soon learn to call the **Violet Aegis**, headquartered beyond the Kingdom's reach, answerable to no throne.

> "Only those who know the duration of the Void may enter the Spire." — inscription above the Astral Spire's first gate

The days of hiding were over.
