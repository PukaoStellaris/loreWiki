The Spire rose out of the borderlands like a needle of {azure}pale blue glass{/}, older than the Kingdom and indifferent to it. The defectors reached it on the ninth day, and the doors — sealed for centuries — opened for {sentinel}Sentinel{/} without being asked.

No one commented on that. Everyone thought about it.

## First Watch

They were still hanging maps in the war room when the Kingdom's answer arrived, shouted up from the valley floor by a rider under a white banner.

:::dialogue
Citadel Herald: By order of the Archon, all former Cardinals are commanded to return to the Citadel and submit to judgment! Compliance within seven days will be met with clemency!
Luna: He sent one herald. Into the borderlands. Alone.
Mirai: Clemency. That's a new word for the gallows.
Luna (softly): We're really not going back, are we.
Mirai: You are home, Luna. It just doesn't have curtains yet.
> Far below, the herald waited, then turned his horse and rode back the way he had come.
:::

---

The decree followed a week later, nailed to every message post in the border towns:

> Let it be known that the assembly styling itself the "Violet Aegis" holds no charter, no rank, and no protection under Kingdom law. Any citizen rendering them aid does so as an accomplice to treason.

{luna}Luna{/} read it aloud in the great hall to general laughter. {gold}Mei Xi{/} framed the first copy. Recruitment, against every reasonable expectation, *tripled*.
